Leavenworth, WA · 3 days

Leavenworth Weekend: A She Goes Outside Guide

All the views. Real trails. Gentle miles.

A riverside loop, a lakeshore hike and a canyon walk across an old iron pipeline bridge, plus an outdoor soak at The Springs, brats and broth bowls, and a hidden cocktail bar. Everything in the right order, so you never backtrack.

Friday

Arrive, stretch your legs on the flat Blackbird Island river loop, then brats and a beer at München Haus.

Saturday

J5 coffee, the Northlake Loop at Lake Wenatchee, shopping and a Gingerbread Factory treat, The Springs, dinner on the Yodelin deck, cocktails at Blind Tigress.

Sunday

Crepes, then the Old Pipeline Bed trail through Tumwater Canyon on your way home.

Scouted in person by JoAnne

I walked every trail and checked every stop in this guide myself in October 2026, and I check the details each season. About 6.4 gentle miles over the whole weekend. Great for friend trips, mother-daughter weekends, solo resets and midweek getaways.
